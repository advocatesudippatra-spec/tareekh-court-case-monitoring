What's new in Tareekh 2.6

Weekly update of next dates, Archive and Flag for every case

Tareekh 2.6 keeps your next dates up to date with the least effort. Instead of checking every case every week, Tareekh lists only the cases whose date has passed without a newer one, and reminds you every Sunday morning. Cases you do not want checked can be archived, and cases the portal does not update can be flagged for a court visit.

🔁 Update due

  • A new list in Cases: every pending case whose date has passed with no newer date, and every case whose date is not known. Oldest first.
  • Update all here opens them one after another; you type each CAPTCHA, and each case is updated.
  • A case updated to a later date leaves the list, and comes back only after that date goes by.
  • A case the portal has not updated stays on the list and comes up again next week by itself.
  • The first time, all the cases with old dates appear together, so the whole diary can be brought up to date at once.

📅 Every Sunday

  • At 9 am on Sunday a notification tells you how many cases need their next date, with an Update all here button.
  • The Notes screen shows a Weekly update card whenever cases are due.
  • Update from the eCourts app's export fills in many next dates at once, without a CAPTCHA; those cases leave the list.

⚑ Flag

  • A switch on every case, for a case whose date the portal does not give.
  • Flagged cases are kept under their own heading, Flagged, for a court visit or a date entered by hand, and are not part of Update all here.

🗄️ Archive

  • A switch on every case you do not want checked regularly.
  • Archived cases are left out of the weekly update, the refresh and the evening checks; a hearing already known still shows on the calendar and in the day's reminders.

📋 Cases screen

  • Views: By date · Update due · Flagged · Pending · Archived · All · Disposed.
  • Refresh pending goes through every pending case of the chosen court.
  • Disposed status follows the court's own words (Disposed, Dismissed, Allowed, Withdrawn and so on), shown as the court wrote it, with Check again on every disposed case.

⌨️ CAPTCHA screen

  • The CAPTCHA picture and the answer box come first, and the box is ready for typing as soon as a new picture appears.

Install: open Tareekh-2.6.apk on your Android phone. It installs over the old version and keeps all your cases.
